Lecture notes

AI lecture and study notes for Windows

Lecture and Study Notes mode listens to a lecture, webinar or recorded class and builds structured notes with key points, definitions and examples.

  • Structured notesKey points, definitions and examples, grouped as the lecture goes on.
  • Your course materialAdd slides, readings or a spreadsheet as reference material (PDF, Excel, CSV, Word and text).
  • ExportCopy the notes or save a Markdown file to keep in your own notes app.
  • Runs on your PCOffline mode keeps audio and notes on your computer.
